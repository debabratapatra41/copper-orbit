06 / GUIDELINES

Consistency is a product feature.

01

Structure before decoration

Use a 4 px spacing grid. Group related controls with 8–16 px gaps; separate sections with 32–48 px. Keep corners at 6–12 px.

02

Clarity at every state

Design default, hover, focus, disabled, loading, empty, success, and error states. Pair status colors with a label or icon.

03

Accessible by default

Use visible labels, keyboard navigation, and clear focus rings. Target WCAG AA contrast and 44 px touch controls.
