What the history needs

  • Time range: quick ranges and a custom picker, with a clear timezone and exact timestamps.
  • Timeline: trips, stops, status changes, geofence crossings, alerts, and data gaps in chronological order.
  • Trip detail: start/end, duration, distance, driver, stops and route replay synchronized with the map.
  • Filtering: event type, severity, driver and trip; preserve filters while navigating.
  • Evidence: event source, freshness and confidence; distinguish estimated from reported values.
  • States: no events, partial telemetry, offline periods, loading and inaccessible history.
  • Export: selected range and filters, subject to permissions and retention policy.

Core fleet-manager workflows

Each workflow starts with a question and leads to a clear next step. These are proposals, not confirmed requirements.

01 / MONITORWhere is my fleet?

Search by vehicle or driver, filter status and location, then locate a vehicle on the map.

02 / INVESTIGATEWhat happened?

Open a vehicle, inspect its timeline, choose a trip and replay the route with events.

03 / TRIAGEWhat needs attention?

Filter by severity, inspect context and acknowledge or assign an exception with a note.

04 / FOLLOW UPWhat should happen next?

Contact the driver or assign a task, confirm the outcome, and keep an audit trail.

Live operations

  • Global clusters that expand on zoom; status, direction and freshness visible without relying on color alone.
  • Compact cards with vehicle, status, speed, driver or last driver, update time and locate action.
  • Combinable search and filters; indicate applied filters, matching counts and reset.
  • Clearly separate last known location from currently reporting; expose offline and stale data.
  • Remember map bounds and filters while opening and closing details.

Vehicle detail and actions

  • Overview: identity, current state, location, last update, driver and relevant telemetry.
  • History: trips, stops, geofences and events with time-based map context.
  • Maintenance and documents: due dates, open issues and service history where data exists.
  • Permissions for contact, export and control actions; confirmation for safety-sensitive changes.
  • Notes, ownership and action history so shifts can hand work over reliably.

Questions to resolve together

Fleet and data

Which vehicle types, telemetry sources, locations and update frequencies should the first release support? What makes a vehicle inactive or stale?

History and privacy

How long is history retained? Who can view driver identity, location and trip playback? What timezone should exports use?

Actions and ownership

Which alerts need acknowledgement, assignment or escalation? Which actions should be available to each role?

Suggested next decision: use Direction 01 as the default fleet view and Direction 02 for vehicle details and history. Add exception-first triage once severity, ownership and data quality are defined.
